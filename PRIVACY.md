### Privacy Policy

Floorgraph is a Frappe app that runs entirely inside your own Frappe/ERPNext site. It:

- Makes no outbound network calls of its own (no analytics, telemetry, or third-party API calls).
- Stores all data (Action Log, Downtime Log, OEE Log, Schedule Slot, etc.) in your site's own database, under your own permission and role rules.
- Follows Frappe's standard document permission model - a user can only see data their role already grants access to.

Since floorgraph has no server of its own and collects nothing outside your Frappe site, there is no data for the app's maintainers to access, store, or share. Your site's own privacy policy and data-retention rules govern the data floorgraph creates.

Questions: open an issue at [github.com/s1dharthpv/floorgraph/issues](https://github.com/s1dharthpv/floorgraph/issues).
