### Support

Bugs and feature requests: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/s1dharthpv/floorgraph/issues).

Before filing, check you're on a supported Frappe version (v15 or v16 - see `README.md`) and include:
- Frappe/ERPNext version
- Steps to reproduce
- Relevant error from the browser console or `bench` logs

No paid support tier exists. This is a community-maintained, MIT-licensed app.
